Soufriere, Saint Lucia — Where the Medicine Comes From

Mount Kailash Rejuvenation Centre sits in the hills above Soufriere on the south-west coast of Saint Lucia, in the shadow of the Pitons and the island's dormant volcano. This corner of the Caribbean is unusual: volcanic ash, sulphur springs and heavy tropical rainfall have produced mineral rich soil that grows botanicals of a potency growers elsewhere struggle to match. The plants we work with — Anamu, Soursop leaf, Vervain, St. John's Bush, Fey Duvan, sea moss from the surrounding reefs — are wildcrafted or grown within a short drive of the centre, then processed on the island rather than shipped abroad as raw material.

Harvesting on foot in the same valleys year after year means the team knows which slopes carry the strongest leaf, which season yields the best root, and when a stand needs to be left alone to recover. Fresh material is cleaned and either dried in shade or steeped immediately, so extracts are made from plants picked days rather than months earlier. Sea moss is harvested from open Atlantic water, sun-dried on racks, and sorted by hand. Nothing is irradiated and nothing is standardised with isolated compounds; the finished bottle is the plant, the menstruum and time.

The Clinical Practice

Mount Kailash is a working clinic first and a producer second. Guests and clients are assessed one to one — history, diet, sleep, elimination, stress, medication, and the patterns that traditional Caribbean bush medicine reads in the body — before any formulation is suggested. Protocols usually begin with cleansing and elimination, move to blood and organ support, and only then add targeted formulas for the presenting concern. Clients are reviewed as they progress, and dosing is adjusted rather than fixed.

The same clinical thinking shapes every product in the apothecary: each formula exists because it was needed in consultation, and most have been used with clients for years before being bottled for the public. Consultations are available in person in Saint Lucia and remotely by video for clients in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and across the Caribbean. Book a consultation or read the library of articles on protocols, herbs and preparation.

Nothing offered here is a diagnosis, and nothing replaces the care of a licensed physician. Formulations support the body's own capacity to cleanse, rebuild and hold balance; anyone pregnant, nursing, or taking prescription medication is asked to consult their doctor before beginning a protocol.

Rt Hon Priest Kailash K Leonce

The centre was founded by Rt Hon Priest Kailash K Leonce, a Saint Lucian herbal physician with more than twenty-one years of clinical practice. Raised in a family and community where bush medicine was ordinary household knowledge, he went on to formalise that inheritance into a clinical system: assessment, protocol, dosage, review. He has since formulated for tens of thousands of clients, trained more than five hundred certified herbal physicians through the School of Wellness Medicine, and hosts free public webinars each month that draw attendees from more than forty countries.

His work has brought international attention to Saint Lucia's botanical wellness tradition, including coverage of the island's wellness travel trail by National Geographic. He teaches, consults and leads the retreats personally, and remains the formulator behind The Answer and every protocol in the apothecary.
