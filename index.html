Mount Kailash Rejuvenation Centre — Clinical Wellness Medicine from Saint Lucia

Mount Kailash Rejuvenation Centre is a Caribbean clinical wellness medicine practice based in Soufriere, Saint Lucia, led by Rt Hon Priest Kailash K Leonce. We craft herbal tinctures, sea moss formulations, and traditional wellness medicine from mineral rich soil botanicals grown in the volcanic highlands of Saint Lucia. Our work spans an apothecary, a wholesale supply line for practitioners, immersive healing retreats, and a school training the next generation of herbal physicians.
